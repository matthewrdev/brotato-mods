# Wish 0.1.1

- Wish for Growth replaces Wish for Treasure. It adds 0.3 percentage points
  to Harvesting growth per wave while held, or 0.6 when cursed.
- Wish for Corruption replaces Wish for Fortune. On purchase, it curses one
  random eligible item or weapon; a cursed copy can curse two. The shop now
  refreshes the inventory and shows which gear was cursed.
- Existing saved Treasure and Fortune item IDs remain compatible with the new
  items.

Requires Abyssal Terrors. Targeted at Brotato 1.1.12.0.beta-3 with ModLoader
6.3.0. Fully quit and restart Brotato after updating.
